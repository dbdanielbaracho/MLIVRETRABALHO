# PSP Public Pricing Snapshot — 2026-09-24

**Status:** indicative public pricing only; not contractual unit economics.  
**Purpose:** reduce uncertainty before commercial due diligence. FIN-RISK remains OPEN.

## Asaas

Public pricing page currently shows standard rates including:

- Pix received: R$ 1,99 per transaction after the promotional period shown on the page;
- boleto received: R$ 1,99 per transaction after the promotional period shown on the page;
- debit card: R$ 0,35 + 1,89%;
- credit card à vista: R$ 0,49 + 2,99%;
- higher rates for installment ranges.

The same page explicitly says displayed rates are standard and that conditions applied to a specific contract/account must be checked in the Asaas account. Therefore these values cannot be treated as marketplace/split commercial terms for MLIVRETRABALHO.

## Pagar.me / Stone

The public offers page shows an Essential offer with public checkout/link rates, but the offer that includes **Split de pagamentos** is presented with **customized rates / talk to a commercial agent**.

Therefore no public number is accepted as the actual split cost for MLIVRETRABALHO. A written commercial proposal is required.

## Mercado Pago

The public checkout page currently displays general online-checkout rates such as:

- Pix: 0,99%;
- credit: rates varying by settlement timing (for example, public values shown for immediate/14-day/30-day availability);
- boleto: fixed public fee shown on the checkout page.

However those public checkout rates do not prove the commercial conditions for Split 1:N. The developer documentation states that 1:N is restricted to advised/managed sellers in contact with the commercial team. Therefore 1:N pricing must be obtained commercially.

## Consequence for MLIVRETRABALHO

Public pricing is insufficient to select a provider or finalize unit economics because the decisive product is marketplace/workforce multi-recipient split.

Required contractual inputs:

1. charge/payment processing fee by method;
2. split/subaccount/recipient fee, if any;
3. payout/transfer fee;
4. settlement timing and optional anticipation cost;
5. refund fee and whether processing fee is returned;
6. chargeback/dispute fee and liability allocation;
7. KYC/KYB/onboarding fee, if any;
8. monthly/minimum-volume/platform/API fees;
9. negotiated volume tiers;
10. tax/documentation impacts confirmed by Brazilian accounting/legal review.

## Stop-the-line

Do not feed the public numbers above into a production pricing commitment. They are only inputs for scenario modeling until written provider quotes exist.
